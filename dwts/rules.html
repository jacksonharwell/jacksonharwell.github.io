1 • Auction Draft

  • Everyone begins with 200 Dancer Bux.
  • An owner nominates any available celebrity. Everyone may bid; highest bid wins.
  • Next owner nominates someone new. Continue until everyone owns 2 dancers.
  • Any Bux you save remain yours all season.

2 • Weekly Scoring

  • Use each dancer's 3 highest individual judge scores. Average them and round down.
  • Highest total judges' score that night: +2. Second-highest: +1.
  • Eliminated that night: -1.
  • Guest-judge scores count.

3 • Lock of the Night

  • Before the show, Lock one of your dancers.
  • Lock finishes Top 3: +2. Eliminated Lock: -1. Otherwise no Lock bonus.

4 • Special Challenges

  • Individually judged dance? Score it normally.
  • Two-dance / instant / fast dances: normal scoring.
  • Relay or dance-off winner: +1.
  • Winning team/group dance: +1 per dancer.
  • Dance Marathon: winner +2, runner-up +1.
  • Official show-awarded bonus points count normally. Weird new format = commissioner ruling.

5 • Dancer Bux & Free Agents

  • Your 200 Bux are one budget for the entire season: Draft, free agents, trades and the Week 6 Shakeup.
  • For a free agent, submit a secret bid. Highest bid gets the dancer and pays that amount.
  • Dancer Bux never reset.

6 • Trades

  • Trade dancers, Dancer Bux, or both between episodes.
  • Only obvious collusion is vetoed.

7 • Week 6 Shakeup

  • After Week 6, all fantasy rosters dissolve.
  • Every surviving celebrity returns to the pool for a live auction using remaining Dancer Bux.
  • Everyone wins one dancer before anyone can win a second.

8 • Finale Showdown

  • Everyone predicts the full final finishing order.
  • Predictions are public: current 1st place submits first, then 2nd, 3rd, down to last.
  • Correct placement bonuses: 1st +8 • 2nd +5 • 3rd +3 • 4th +2.
  • Season points carry into the finale.

League Recordkeeping

The commissioner records every auction purchase, weekly score, Lock, waiver claim, trade and standings change. This public site is the shared league ledger so everyone can see exactly how the current standings were built.

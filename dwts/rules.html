1 • Auction Draft

  • Everyone begins with 200 Dancer Bux.
  • An owner nominates any available celebrity. Everyone may bid; highest bid wins.
  • Next owner nominates someone new. Continue until everyone owns 2 dancers.
  • Any Bux you save remain yours all season.

2 • Weekly Scoring

  • Use each dancer's 3 highest individual judge scores. For Weeks 1–5, average them and round down. Beginning with Week 6, average them, round up and double that rounded base score.
  • Only the rounded weekly base score doubles in the second half. Judges-leaderboard bonuses, special-challenge bonuses and elimination penalties keep their listed values. Locks ended after Week 5.
  • Highest total judges' score that night: +2. Second-highest: +1.
  • Eliminated that night: -1.
  • Guest-judge scores count.

3 • Locks retired after Week 5

  • Beginning Week 6, there are no Lock selections, Lock bonus points, or Lock penalties.
  • Previously awarded Lock points and penalties from Weeks 1–5 remain in the historical standings.

4 • Special Challenges

  • Individually judged dance? Score it normally.
  • Two-dance / instant / fast dances: normal scoring.
  • Relay or dance-off winner: +1.
  • Winning team/group dance: +1 per dancer.
  • Dance Marathon: winner +2, runner-up +1.
  • Official show-awarded bonus points count normally. Weird new format = commissioner ruling.

5 • Dancer Bux & Free Agents

  • Effective October 20, 2026 (Week 6), all Dancer Bux balances are zero and remain zero through the end of the season.
  • No further Bux are earned, spent or transferred. Prior Bux transactions remain in the historical record.
  • Unowned surviving dancers remain available for commissioner-recorded claims without Bux payments.

6 • Trades

  • Trade dancers between episodes. Dancer Bux transfers ended October 20, 2026.
  • Only obvious collusion is vetoed.

7 • Week 6 Shakeup

  • Immediately before the Week 6 episode, all fantasy rosters dissolve. Every owner keeps all points earned during Weeks 1–5.
  • Every surviving celebrity returns to the selection pool. The Bux selection wheel determines who chooses next—not an auction—and each owner selects exactly one dancer.
  • Each current Dancer Bux gives one equally likely ticket, randomly scattered around the wheel. After an owner wins a pick, all of that owner’s tickets are removed. After a dancer is selected, that dancer is removed from the available pool. Bux are not spent by spinning; owners with zero Bux have no wheel tickets.
  • The shakeup ends after all six owners have selected one dancer. Any surviving dancers left over remain unowned.

8 • Finale Showdown

  • Everyone predicts the full final finishing order.
  • Predictions are public: current 1st place submits first, then 2nd, 3rd, down to last.
  • Correct placement bonuses: 1st +8 • 2nd +5 • 3rd +3 • 4th +2.
  • Season points carry into the finale.

League Recordkeeping

The commissioner records every auction purchase, weekly score, Lock, waiver claim, trade and standings change. This public site is the shared league ledger so everyone can see exactly how the current standings were built.
